I'm **Aska** — a solo developer who builds production AI systems from West Java, Indonesia.

My background is Islamic Economics, not Computer Science. I started coding in 2025 through AI-assisted development, and somehow ended up running infrastructure that real businesses depend on. That gap — between where I started and what I now build — is what drives everything I write.

## What I Build

**ORCA** is a multi-tenant WhatsApp AI customer service platform I built and run solo. Three active property tenants. Real sales conversations, daily. Runs on a VPS with **$0/month** in AI costs — through provider rotation across Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, and OpenRouter, with a circuit breaker that dropped median response time from 17 seconds to under 2.

I also built **AMS**, a full property ERP on CodeIgniter 4 — 49 users, 16 roles, 48 modules. And a few open-source packages on Packagist along the way.

## How I Work

Stable boring technology. Pragmatic incremental delivery. I don't build demos — I build things that run in production with real users depending on them.

My formal title at work doesn't cover half of what I actually do. That's fine. The systems speak for themselves.

## What I Write About

Most AI content is either too expensive to replicate or too abstract to apply. I write from the middle — from someone who actually runs this stuff, with limited budget and no team:

Things like running AI infrastructure at near-zero cost. Building systems that don't break when one provider goes down. Using AI for creative work without losing the brand. And figuring out, one project at a time, what "using AI seriously" actually looks like in practice.
