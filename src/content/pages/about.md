I'm **Aska** (Asep Iskandar), a solo developer and digital marketer based in **West Java, Indonesia**.

My formal title doesn't cover half of what I actually do — spanning ERP development, AI system architecture, marketing, data analysis, and content creation across two property companies.

## What I Build

My flagship project is **ORCA** — a multi-tenant WhatsApp AI customer service platform I built and run solo. It handles real property sales conversations across multiple tenants, sits on a VPS, and runs on **$0/month** in AI costs through smart provider rotation and circuit breaker architecture.

I also built **AMS**, a full property ERP system on CodeIgniter 4 with a 49-user, 16-role RBAC system across 48 modules — and several other tools including open-source PHP packages published to Packagist.

## How I Work

I'm self-taught, starting from an Islamic Economics background and building my first production system through AI-assisted development in 2025. My philosophy is simple: **stable boring technology, pragmatic incremental delivery, and app-level constraints over architectural purity.**

I don't build demos. I build things that run in production with real users depending on them.

## What I Write About

- **AI cost optimization** — running real systems on minimal budget
- **Production AI architecture** — what actually works vs. what sounds good
- **AI workflows for creators** — prompting, creative systems, and building on-brand output
- **Building as a solo operator** — doing more with less, faster than a team
- **The efficient AI ecosystem** — tools, stacks, and habits that compound over time

Whether you're a founder, a marketer, a developer, or just someone trying to integrate AI into your work without overpaying — you're in the right place.
